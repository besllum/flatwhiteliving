Services

One point of contact. A considered design. A project that is taken care of.

Every project is different.

Some clients need help shaping a complete interior. Others have already found their home and need someone to take care of the renovation. A few want a design drawn up from scratch, and others just want a trusted pair of eyes to bring everything together.

I offer a flexible, personal service that can adapt to what your project actually needs.

Interior Design Architecture Project Management Styling Property Staging

01

Interior Design

A home should feel personal rather than prescribed.

I develop interiors around your lifestyle, your taste and the character of your property, bringing together spatial planning, colour, materials, furniture, lighting and finishing details.

From the initial concept to the final installation, every element is considered as part of the whole.

02

Architecture

Sometimes the best interior starts by changing the space itself.

I can help develop architectural solutions that improve the layout, flow and functionality of your home, working with the existing property and the possibilities within it.

Where specialist architectural or technical expertise is required, I coordinate with the appropriate professionals as part of the wider project.

03

Project Management

This is where design becomes reality.

I coordinate the moving parts of your project, from suppliers and contractors to deliveries and installation, keeping communication clear and the design on track.

For international clients in particular, having someone locally who can be present, follow up and deal with the details can make a significant difference.

You can be involved as much or as little as you wish.

04

Styling

The final layer is what makes a house feel like home.

I select and arrange furniture, artwork, lighting, textiles and objects to create an interior that feels considered, comfortable and personal.

05

Property Staging

First impressions matter.

For properties coming to market, I create a considered presentation that highlights the potential of the space and helps prospective buyers or tenants see themselves living there.

A service tailored to your project

You may need one service or the complete process.

We will define the right scope together at the beginning, so you have a clear understanding of what I will take care of and what you can expect from the process.
