A softer expression of grandeur

Residential · The Hague

The brief

The brief was to refresh the furnishings of this beautiful home in the Visserijbuurt in The Hague, creating a warmer and more inviting atmosphere while respecting the grandeur of its original architecture.

With its generous proportions, high ceilings and beautiful cornices and mouldings, the house had a strong architectural presence. The challenge was to introduce a sense of softness and intimacy, making the interiors feel comfortable and welcoming for everyday family life without compromising their elegance.

The focus was on rethinking the living and dining areas, carefully balancing furniture, lighting and accessories to bring harmony and warmth to the generous spaces.

The design

The living and dining rooms form one long, connected space. To create a more balanced arrangement, I introduced two lounge chairs as a subtle visual divider. They help define the living area while keeping the overall layout open and allowing circulation to remain effortless.

An extra-large rug was essential to anchor the living room furniture and bring the different elements together. With children and a dog in the family, I selected a washable, low-pile rug that combines practicality with a refined appearance.

Although the room benefits from generous windows, its considerable depth meant that natural light did not always reach the centre of the space. Two mirrored side tables help reflect light and add a sense of brightness. Rather than relying on overhead lighting, I chose an elegant arc floor lamp to bring light closer to the seating area, creating a warmer and more intimate atmosphere.

In the dining room, the existing chairs were updated, while a vintage credenza and carefully selected mirror introduced character and a more collected feel. Cushions, accessories and new window treatments, combining Roman blinds and velvet curtains, added further layers of softness and texture.

The garden room was also given a new purpose, with a dedicated area for the children to play and enjoy, ensuring the home feels as practical as it is elegant.

Materials and details

The material palette was chosen to soften the architectural grandeur and bring warmth to the interiors. Velvet, linen and natural wood introduce a tactile quality, balancing the more formal character of the original architecture with a relaxed and comfortable atmosphere.

Mirrored surfaces help reflect the available light, while carefully selected accessories and vintage pieces add depth, personality and a sense of individuality.

Every element was considered to create a home that feels refined yet relaxed, where beautiful architectural details coexist naturally with the rhythms of family life.
