How it works

From first idea to finished interior.

A successful project starts with a good conversation.

Before thinking about colours, furniture or finishes, I want to understand the property, the possibilities and what you want the space to become.

{{ placeholderLabel }}

Proceso: planos, materiales, obra

01

Let's Talk

We begin with a conversation about your property, your lifestyle, your ambitions and the scope of the project. It is an opportunity to understand what you need and for you to understand how I work.

02

Define the Project

We establish the scope, priorities, responsibilities and the level of involvement you would like from Flat White Living.

03

The Design

The concept becomes tangible. Plans, layouts, materials, furniture, lighting and bespoke elements are developed into a coherent design that can be clearly visualised before implementation begins.

04

Manage & Deliver

The design moves from paper into the real world. Depending on the scope, I coordinate with architects, contractors, craftspeople and suppliers to ensure the design is executed with care and consistency.

05

The Final Layer

Furniture, artwork, textiles, lighting and personal objects are carefully placed to bring the interior together. The finished space should feel considered — but never as though it has been overworked.

You decide how involved you want to be.

For international clients, this can mean having a trusted local person take care of the day-to-day details while you remain involved in the decisions that matter most.
