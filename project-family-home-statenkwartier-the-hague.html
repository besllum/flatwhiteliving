A more connected family home in Statenkwartier

Residential · The Hague

The brief

After several years of living in their home following their return to the Netherlands, a family of four felt it was time to rethink the layout and make better use of the available space.

The kitchen was relatively small and lacked sufficient storage, while the living room was generous and filled with natural light. The challenge was to create a more functional and connected living environment, making better use of the home’s proportions while preserving the character of its different spaces.

The aim was to bring more light into the kitchen, improve storage and create a natural connection between cooking, dining and living. The renovation also included the master bathroom, where the intention was to create a warm and calming space.

The design

The kitchen and dining area were brought together through a newly designed peninsula, creating a natural connection between the two spaces while maintaining a sense of separation from the formal dining area.

The peninsula became the heart of the home, transforming the way the family uses the space. Breakfasts, light lunches and afternoon snacks now naturally bring everyone together around the kitchen. The new layout also improved the circulation throughout the home, creating a more intuitive flow between the different living areas.

Floor-to-ceiling cabinetry maximises storage while keeping the kitchen clean and visually balanced. By opening up the layout, the kitchen now benefits from the generous natural light entering through the living room, making it feel brighter and more spacious.

The master bathroom was redesigned with the same attention to atmosphere and functionality. A combination of natural textures, warm earthy tones and carefully selected materials creates a calm and inviting space.

Materials and details

The material palette was chosen to complement the existing character of the home, continuing its warm, understated aesthetic through stone-inspired surfaces, beige tones and natural finishes.

In the kitchen, solid oak flooring brings warmth and continuity to the living spaces, while small-format mosaic tiles define the cooking area, offering a practical and easy-to-maintain surface without compromising on the overall aesthetic.

In the master bathroom, textured stone-coloured wall tiles add depth and subtle character, balanced by simple ceramic floor tiles. An oak vanity introduces natural warmth, while softly diffused wall lighting creates an intimate and relaxed atmosphere. Marble and natural stone basins add variation in texture and a refined finishing touch.

The result is a home that feels more connected, functional and welcoming, where thoughtful spatial changes and a considered material palette have transformed not only the spaces, but also the way the family lives in them.
