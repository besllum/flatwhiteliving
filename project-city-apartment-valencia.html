A city apartment in Valencia

International · Valencia

The brief

A pre-war apartment in the centre of Valencia, bought remotely by clients based in the Netherlands. The whole project — survey, design, suppliers and site — was run at a distance, with the owners visiting twice.

The approach

The original tiled floors were restored where they survived and matched where they did not, and the ceiling heights were left entirely alone. New work was concentrated in the kitchen and the two bathrooms so the period rooms could stay as they were.

Shade was designed in: shutters, deep reveals and layered curtains let the apartment stay cool through the summer without darkening the rooms.

Pavimentos hidráulicos recuperados, sombra proyectada y obra nueva concentrada en cocina y baños.

Materials and details

Restored hydraulic tile, lime-washed walls, cane and pale timber, with an unlacquered brass that is allowed to darken. Everything was sourced locally so the apartment belongs to its city.
