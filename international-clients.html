For international clients

Creating a home in the Netherlands, without having to navigate it alone.

Moving to another country brings enough decisions with it.

Renovating or furnishing a home shouldn't have to become another full-time job.

At Flat White Living, I work with international clients who want a beautiful, personal home in the Netherlands and someone they can trust to look after the process locally.

From design and sourcing to contractors, suppliers and installation, I can be involved as much as you need.

A local partner you can trust

You may not know which supplier to call, how a renovation normally works here or who to contact when something doesn't go according to plan. You shouldn't have to.

I act as your local point of contact, helping you navigate the project and keeping communication between everyone involved clear and organised.

You don't have to be in the Netherlands every day.

If you travel for work, live between countries, or would rather not spend your evenings coordinating a renovation, I can handle the day-to-day details for you.

You remain involved in the decisions that matter. I take care of the rest.

Design that feels personal

Being an international client doesn't mean your home should feel generic.

I take the time to understand your lifestyle, your taste and the things that make a place feel like home to you.

The interior is designed around your life, not around a predetermined style.
